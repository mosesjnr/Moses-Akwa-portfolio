About

I connect analysis with action.

My work sits at the intersection of data analysis, predictive modelling, technology and practical problem solving. I enjoy taking an unclear question, working through the data, identifying meaningful patterns and turning the result into something useful.

My earlier data projects cover loan eligibility, hotel booking cancellations, Lagos property rent prediction and wine quality classification. More recent software work extends that problem-solving approach into healthcare workflows, e-commerce and community media platforms.

I value clear thinking, structured workflows, maintainable technology and solutions that can be understood and used by the people they are intended to serve.

ANALYTICAL MINDSET

EDA, modelling, feature relationships, evaluation and insight generation.

OPERATIONS MINDSET

Structured workflows, practical constraints, process thinking and useful outputs.
